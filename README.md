# joshMeeks.github.io
Hey I am Josh Meeks, I am in my final semester of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Nevada, Reno. Teamwork, teamwork, teamwork. If there is one thing I have learned throughout many stressful projects is that team work really does make the dream work. A good team member has your back when you struggle and is able to push you when you need motivation. Any tough job is a 1000x easier when you have your team by your side. At some point I looked up at the stars at night and got goosebumps thinking about the possibilities and unknowns above my head. While I may never get to see the stars myself, I can at least put all of my efforts towards the making rocket propulsion safer and more efficient. I have been working with various monopropellant and bipropellant solid and liquid rockets and gas generators for nearly two years now and I hope to continue down this path.
